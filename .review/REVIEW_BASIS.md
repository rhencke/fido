## Accepted claim

M2 delivers one small diagnostic timing aid: `make perf` runs the exact `make -j1 check` path once
project-cold and once hot on one dedicated serial builder, records cumulative elapsed milliseconds at nine
real target completions, and replaces one tracked `.review/PERFORMANCE.tsv`. `git diff` is the comparison.

Timing is diagnostic evidence. It is not certified correctness, not a semantic authority, not a benchmark
framework and not an acceptance gate: no gate consults it and nothing depends on it.

## Blocking defect classes

A candidate is blocked when any of these holds.

- **The tracked record claims something the run did not do.** The builder it names is not the builder Make
  used; the clock arithmetic does not match the stated resolution; a toolchain was acquired inside an
  interval the header calls excluded; a checkpoint row is absent, duplicated, or attributed to the wrong
  mode.
- **Publication is not atomic.** The record changes after a failed cold run, a failed hot run, a failed
  write, a failed mode change, or a rename that was really a copy across filesystems.
- **The timer became a framework.** A registry, configuration language, resume, selector, comparison
  implementation, statistical analysis, self-test framework, local bundle hierarchy or schema appears — or
  any deleted abstraction returns under a new name.
- **The timer acquires authority.** `make perf` becomes a prerequisite of anything, or a gate reads
  `.review/PERFORMANCE.tsv`.
- **Residue of the withdrawn experiment survives.** Any implementation, data, control, compatibility path,
  exemption or prose surface of it remains in the live tree.
- **A correctness path moved.** Proof, extraction, transport, generated bytes, goldens, runtime behaviour,
  the no-host-Python boundary, or ordinary `make check` and pre-commit parallelism changed.
- **Complexity does not fit.** Retained machinery mainly supports itself rather than the product, contrary
  to `D-30`.

## Required evidence

- `.review/PERFORMANCE.tsv` from one real run, naming `fido-perf-v1`, one project-cold and one immediate hot
  `make -j1 check`, with the nine completion rows exactly once per mode.
- `git diff -- .review/PERFORMANCE.tsv` as the whole comparison mechanism.
- `docker buildx inspect fido-perf-v1` agreeing with the header's serial claim.
- The Git diff as the deletion evidence; no closure audit or deletion ledger.
- Every permanent gate green, with its own controls, and every retained mutation still load-bearing.
- `make check`, `make regenerate`, `make regen-guard`, `make audit-fresh`, and the real pre-commit hook over
  the exact staged snapshot without bypass.
- Generated `go.mod` and every generated `.go` byte-identical; the whole-theory assumption audit green.

findings keep their M3 and M4 owners and are recorded in `.review/M_SERIES_PLAN.md`. The exact M4 plan
remains forbidden until M3 finishes and Rob accepts that plan.
